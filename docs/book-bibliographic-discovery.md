
# Book / Bibliographic Discovery Layer — v2.2.0

v2.2.0 adds metadata-first book and bibliographic discovery planning and classification.

## Purpose

Book references can support historical context, topic context, claim support, bibliography work, and research direction.

This layer maps book, ISBN, catalog, preview, archive, and bibliographic candidates into the Broad Reference Result Model with `source_class: book`.

## Supported metadata targets

- ISBN records
- catalog entries
- Open Library records
- Google Books preview records
- WorldCat records
- Archive.org scans
- publisher pages
- library records
- bibliography entries

## Safety boundaries

- No copyrighted text extraction
- No full-text scraping
- No paywall bypass
- No access circumvention
- No copying large copyrighted passages
- No claim of legal permission certainty
- No export expansion beyond metadata at this milestone

## Product rule

Discovery first. Classification second. Risk/access/licensing labels third. User judgment fourth. Creative or research activation last.

Book references are metadata, catalog, preview, archive, and bibliography objects unless access and rights are reviewed.
